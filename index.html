2. Skincare: How to Build a Simple Daily Routine

Building a skincare routine does not have to mean buying lots of expensive products. The best place to start is with a few everyday essentials that suit your skin and fit comfortably into your budget.

Your morning skincare routine

  • Gentle cleanser: Choose a cleanser that removes dirt and excess oil without leaving your skin feeling tight or uncomfortable.
  • Moisturiser: Look for a formula that helps keep your skin hydrated. Lightweight lotions may suit people who prefer a less heavy feel, while creams can offer a richer texture.
  • Sunscreen: Choose a broad-spectrum sunscreen with SPF 30 or higher. Consider the texture and finish you enjoy so that you are more likely to use it consistently.

Your evening skincare routine

  • Remove makeup and sunscreen: Use a suitable makeup remover or gentle cleansing product.
  • Cleanse: Wash away the day's dirt, oil and product build-up without scrubbing harshly.
  • Moisturise: Apply a suitable moisturiser to help maintain your skin's hydration.

How to choose skincare products

Before purchasing a skincare product, consider your skin's needs, the ingredients, the product texture and your budget. A popular product is not automatically the right choice for everyone.

  • For oily skin: You may prefer lightweight, non-comedogenic moisturisers.
  • For dry skin: Consider richer moisturisers with ingredients such as glycerin or ceramides.
  • For sensitive skin: Look for gentle formulas and consider fragrance-free options.
  • For combination skin: Choose products that balance comfort and hydration without feeling too heavy.

What to consider before buying

When comparing skincare products, look beyond attractive packaging and marketing claims. Check the ingredient list, product size, directions for use and suitability for your skin. Compare prices from reputable retailers and read product reviews critically.

You do not need an expensive routine to get started. A few carefully chosen essentials are often more practical than purchasing several products you may not need.

Give your skin time

Introduce new products gradually and patch-test when appropriate. Avoid changing your entire routine whenever a new trend appears. Stop using a product if it causes irritation. For persistent skin concerns, consult a qualified healthcare professional or dermatologist.

The most useful skincare routine is one you understand, enjoy and can maintain consistently.
